# Cabin Visualiser

Interactive 1:1 layout tool for a [Dunster House Terminator pent log cabin](https://dunsterhouse.co.uk/log-cabins/pent/terminator-log-cabin-w5-0m-x-d4-0m) (W5.0m × D4.0m) with real-scale furniture.

## Cabin

- External: 4.99m × 3.99m
- Internal: 4.77m × 3.77m (18.00 m²)
- Walls: 45mm interlocking spruce logs
- Floor / roof: 19mm T&G
- Pent roof: front eaves 2.30m, back eaves 1.90m, ridge 2.49m
- Front: narrow window + French doors + two narrow windows, anthracite frames
- Door walkthrough: 1.11m × 1.82m
- Window opening: 0.44m × 1.37m

## Furniture (real sizes — do not shrink)

- Corner sofa 236 × 152 cm, charcoal
- Marcy multi-gym 102 × 185 × 208 cm
- Two IKEA KALLAX 77 × 77 × 39 cm, side by side
- Desk 120 × 60 cm with monitor and office chair
- 55" TV (123 × 71 cm) on the TRIOBLADE 200 × 35 × 45 cm high-gloss LED unit
- Compact bar 112 × 48 × 105 cm, mini fridge, 3 stools
